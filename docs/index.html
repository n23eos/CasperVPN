Blocked tonight. Back by morning.

CasperVPN is an open-source backend for running a subscription VPN that survives state-level DPI filtering — the TSPU/RKN class. Traffic mimics allowed HTTPS and HTTP-3. Nodes rotate when blocked. A feedback loop turns “what got blocked where” into new configs and domains.

sing-box on every node. Clients are external — Happ or any sing-box-compatible app — connected through a per-user subscription URL. No custom apps for a store to purge.

Survival is diversity plus feedback, not stronger encryption

1.0

Many transports, no monoculture

Every node carries VLESS-REALITY, Hysteria2, Shadowsocks-2022 and AmneziaWG at once. The client switches on its own — the censor has to kill four protocols to kill one node.

2.0

Field signals

Clients report anonymous field signals — no PII by construction. The system learns what got blocked, where, and on which carrier, while users stay unidentifiable.

3.0

Automatic replacement

The orchestrator detects the block, provisions a fresh node with Terraform and Ansible, rotates IPs and mimicry domains. Entry and exit are separate machines — attacking an entry never exposes the exit.
